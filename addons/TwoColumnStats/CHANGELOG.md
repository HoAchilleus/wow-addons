## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-20

### Added

- Shows the character sheet's stats in two columns, so nothing needs scrolling.
- Widens the character window to fit, and puts it back on other tabs.
- Extends the stats pane's background art with mirror images, so there are no seams.
- Rows grow to fill the pane, snapped to whole screen pixels so spacing is even.
- In combat, stats the game hides from addons keep their last value instead of disappearing.
- `/tcs` toggles between two columns and the default sheet.
- `/tcs debug` lists the textures at the background crest, for troubleshooting.
